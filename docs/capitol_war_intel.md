# Z Route: Redemption — Capitol War Mechanics & S117 Operational Intel

Internal reference documenting verified Capitol War game mechanics, Server 117 governance rules, roster context, and tactical lessons from the 2026-11-07 defense against Server 121.

## 1. Battlefield & Combat Mechanics

### Objectives: Capitol & Four Large Turrets (Guns)
* The **Capitol** sits at the center of the mud zone and is surrounded by **four capturable Large Turrets (Guns)** that directly impact objective control and battlefield attrition.
* **Cross-Alliance Garrisoning:** Any commander from the **same server** can garrison/reinforce the Capitol or any friendly-held Turret, **regardless of alliance tag**. Building reinforcement is server-wide, not restricted to the holding alliance.

### Mud Zone vs. Grass Perimeter
* **No Shields in Mud:** Bases positioned inside the mud zone immediately surrounding the Capitol and Turrets **cannot activate shields**.
* **Severe Mud March Slowdown:** Troops march very slowly across the mud. Sending marches or reinforcements from the grass perimeter takes a long time compared to short point-blank hops from inside the mud.
* **Grass Garrisoning Preserves Shields:** Reinforcing/garrisoning the Capitol or Turrets from the grass **does not break a player's shield** and **does not trigger War Frenzy**.
* **War Frenzy Duration:** Direct base/tile combat triggers War Frenzy, which blocks shielding for too long to wait out during an active battle window.

### Wall Defense & Graveyard Bleed
* **Empty Wall Penalty:** Leaving a base's wall defense empty (unchecked squads) causes every incoming hit on that base to send **~250–450 soldiers directly to the Graveyard** (permanent troop loss), **skipping the Hospital entirely**.
* Consequently, mud-parked bases cannot simply uncheck wall defenses to protect their troops while garrisoning objectives without suffering permanent troop bleed on every hit.

### Teleportation & Cross-Server Repositioning Loop
* **Free Cross-Server Entry:** Teleporting from the visiting/external server onto a chosen tile on the battle server is **free**.
* **In-Server Repositioning Cost:** Moving directly to a chosen tile within the same server requires an **Advanced Teleport**, which becomes prohibitively expensive over a two-hour battle.
* **The Escape & Free Re-Entry Loop:**
  1. When a base in the mud is targeted by a whale or focused down by a group, the player uses a cheap **Random Teleport** to immediately escape the mud.
  2. The player then **teleports to the external server (free)**.
  3. From the external server, the player **teleports back into a chosen position in the Capitol mud (free)**.
  * *Trade-off:* While free, the cross-server round trip is much slower than an instant Advanced Teleport, creating a timing window where whichever side has more players already hovering on the external server claims newly vacated mud tiles first.

## 2. Server 117 Governance & Roster Context

### 1.0M Point Threshold (Capitol Buffs & NAP)
* **Capitol Buff Whitelist (Apparatchik):** The **1,000,000 alliance points** minimum in Capitol War has long been the threshold for home-server alliances to be whitelisted for **Capitol buffs** (Cabinet Minister positions).
* **NAP Policy Update (Late Oct / Early Nov 2026):** Server 117 updated its **Non-Aggression Pact (NAP)** policy so that **NAP protection also requires reaching the 1,000,000 alliance points minimum** in Capitol War.
* Following the NAP rule change, qualifying S117 alliances (≥1.0M points) expanded from 9–10 in prior events to **14 alliances** on 2026-11-07 (`P1MP`, `GDI`, `HDG`, `KRS`, `Bigg`, `AZT`, `ALIN`, `P2MP`, `1Lr`, `TIOX`, `KRS2`, `VFD`, `DGe`, `UOU`), with active combatants (≥50k points) reaching an all-time S117 high of **304 commanders** and **326.19M total S117 points**.

### Population & Roster Realities
* **Closed New-Player Funnel:** Game onboarding directs brand-new accounts to much newer servers rather than established servers like S117, meaning S117 operates with a fixed/maturing population pool where some older accounts have quit the game.
* **Attendance vs. Full Roster Size:** Leaderboard participant counts only reflect commanders who logged in and scored during the Saturday window—not full alliance membership rosters (up to 100 members).
* **Alliance Wings:** `[P1MP]` operates **`[P2MP]` (`NowaPolandia`)** as its secondary/wing alliance.

### Known S117 Account Handovers / Renames
* **`Azethoth` (`[GDI]`) → `Tony-Soprano` (`[GDI]`):** `Azethoth` quit the game and handed over the account, which was renamed to `Tony-Soprano` (ranked #2 overall on 2026-11-07 with 17.19M points).

## 3. 2026-11-07 S117 vs. S121 Tactical After-Action Notes

* **S117 Tactical Setup:** S117 focused on holding **three Guns and the Capitol**, surrounding the guns with allied bases in the mud with full wall defenses active.
* **How S121 Broke the Rings:**
  * Server 121 fielded **667 total commanders** (**639 active ≥50k**, including **371 heavy hitters scoring 1M–5M** vs. S117's 89 in that bracket).
  * S121 systematically focused individual S117 bases in the gun rings to force them out (via wall burn or Random Teleport escape).
  * Because S121 had hundreds of players cycling the free cross-server teleport loop, S121 continuously streamed fresh bases into every vacated mud tile faster than S117 players could complete the slow free cross-server hop back in, gradually saturating the Capitol mud and exhausting S117's top accounts by `13:24` server time.
