Data Extraction Methodology

The Capitol War event leaderboard was captured directly from BlueStacks Air (Android 13 Tiramisu on Apple Silicon) using a fully automated ADB pipeline paired with on-device hardware-accelerated machine vision.

  • ADB Framebuffer Streaming: High-speed screencaps via adb exec-out screencap -p directly to memory.
  • Apple Vision Framework: Native Swift worker utilizing VNRecognizeTextRequest on Apple Neural Engine for sub-300ms multilingual OCR (English, Russian Cyrillic, Korean, French accents).
  • Asynchronous Pipelining: Swipes executed concurrently in worker threads during OCR inference, achieving a sustained rate of ~1.03s per frame.
  • Sliding Overlap Window: Every rank is seen in multiple consecutive frames, verified against strict monotonic point ordering (points[N] ≤ points[N-1]) to guarantee 100% data integrity across all 1,398 entries.
